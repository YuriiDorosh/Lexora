YouTube Vocab Radar (M34)

Cooldown is the minimum gap between two auto-pauses, starting when you dismiss the alert — keeps the radar from pausing every other sentence on long videos. Look-ahead is how many seconds before a word the radar pauses, so you have time to read the alert. Your YouTube watch history stays in the browser — Lexora only learns that you have certain words saved.
